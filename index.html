    How Ignite works

    Ignite tracks what Vichy and six competitors (La Roche-Posay, Avène, Bioderma, CeraVe, Eucerin, Klorane) publish on their public Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. Every number comes from those public pages: likes, comments and views as shown on each post, and followers as shown on each profile. Nothing is estimated or made up. Sections that aren't tracked yet say so.

    How to read it: Instagram engagement is likes divided by followers; TikTok and YouTube engagement is likes and comments divided by views. The two aren't compared directly. Share of engagement is each brand's share of all likes on the tracked posts. Times are Paris time. The tool is free and built only on public data.

    Ignite is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Vichy Laboratoires, L'Oréal or any brand shown. Brand names belong to their owners. All data comes from public sources.
